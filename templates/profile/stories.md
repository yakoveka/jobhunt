# STAR story bank

> Interview-ready stories built only from evidence. The agent picks 2–3 per application (in `fit.md`) and uses them
> for screening answers and cover letters. Number them (S1, S2…) so fit notes can refer to them.
> Aim for 6–10 stories that cover: a technical win, a failure/incident, conflict or disagreement, leadership or
> mentoring, ambiguity, delivering under pressure. Mark claims [E] / [I] as in the profile.

## S1: Billing service split (Acme)
- **Situation**: the billing monolith timed out under month-end load [E: incident tickets]
- **Task**: I owned the redesign as the senior backend engineer
- **Action**: carved out three services behind a feature flag, migrated tenants in batches, kept a rollback path
- **Result**: p95 900 ms → 250 ms [E: dashboard export]; zero lost invoices during the migration [I]
- **Lesson**: what you'd do differently
