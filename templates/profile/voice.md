# Voice guide

> How you write, so cover letters and form answers sound like you and not like a template.
> Best built from real samples: paste 3–5 things you wrote yourself (an email you were happy with, a past cover
> letter, a LinkedIn post) and let `/jobs setup` describe the patterns, then edit. Update it whenever you rewrite a
> draft: the "Learned from edits" list is where the agent improves.

## Samples
Short excerpts of your own writing (or paths to them).

## Rules for drafts
1. Open with context: why this company and this role, in one concrete sentence. Never "I am writing to apply…"
2. One concrete piece of evidence per paragraph (a number, a system, a result)
3. Be candid about one real gap and how you'd close it
4. Plain words; short and long sentences mixed; no buzzword chains
5. End with availability and a simple next step

## Never
- "I am thrilled / passionate / excited to…", "In today's fast-paced world", "leverage", "synergy"
- Em-dash chains, rhetorical triplets, a summary paragraph that repeats the letter
- Claims that aren't in the profile
